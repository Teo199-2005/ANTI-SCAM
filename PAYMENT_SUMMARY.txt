Anti-ScamPH Subscription Billing (2026-05-18)
============================================

PLANS
- standard: FREE — Verified Resort — up to 10 active rooms — active on owner signup
- business_pro: PHP 1,000/month — Premium Verified Resort — up to 20 rooms — Xendit monthly invoice/recurring

CHECKOUT
- POST /api/v1/resort-owner/subscriptions/pay-invoice
- billing_scope: monthly only (room_addon removed)
- Charge: PHP 1,000 × 1 month (or pilot flat amount)
- Invoice plan tag: business_pro_m1 or business_pro_m1_rec

DOWNGRADE
- Business Pro overdue → grace_period → downgrade to standard (stay listed)
- php artisan subscriptions:enforce-grace (scheduled)

MIGRATION (existing data)
- php artisan migrate
- php artisan subscriptions:migrate-anti-scamph
